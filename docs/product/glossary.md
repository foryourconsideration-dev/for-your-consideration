# Glossary

## Article

A piece of editorial content, regardless of lifecycle status.

## Article, archived

An article that was previously published but is no longer publicly accessible.

## Article, draft

An article that has never been published and is not publicly accessible.

## Article, published

An article currently available to readers.

## Frontmatter

Structured fields at the beginning of a local Markdown file, separated from the
article body by `---` delimiters.

## Slug

The unique lowercase identifier used in an article's URL.
